About Tamara

Twenty years of faces,
and still counting.

Tamara Boyd has built her career in two worlds at once — the quiet intensity of a wedding morning, and the pace of a national beauty set. That range is the point. A bride gets an artist who has worked under studio lights for brands that photograph skin for a living.

Her work has appeared on the cover of Hype Hair, across two editorials in MALVIE French Magazine, and in the Black Radiance Face of 2025 campaign. She works on location throughout Los Angeles, the Inland Empire and the wider Southern California area.
